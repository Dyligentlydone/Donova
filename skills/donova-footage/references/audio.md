# Sound: SFX and music

## SFX library

`donova add sfx/<name>` copies a licensed sound into `assets/sfx/`.
`donova catalog` lists what's installed. The bundled pack is
self-synthesized (CC0-equivalent — generated by us, safe to ship in products).

Catalog: `whoosh`, `whoosh-soft`, `pop`, `click`, `tick`, `riser`, `impact`,
`thud`, `error`, `chime`, `notification`, `glitch`, `camera-shutter`,
`swish`, `type`.

## Placing SFX

```html
<audio src="assets/sfx/impact.mp3" data-start="11.2" data-volume="0.8"
  data-has-audio="true"></audio>
```

- Timed `<audio>` elements take `data-start`/`data-duration`; they sit inside
  `.clip`s for organization or at root level — audio carries no visuals.
- Hits land ON the moment: the frame the card pops, the cut happens, the word
  lands. Offset by −30–60ms so the peak meets the visual beat.
- Rule of thumb: whoosh/swish on transitions & zooms, pop/click on card pops,
  impact/thud on big beats, error on danger words, riser before reveals,
  chime/notification on wins, type on caption starts (rarely — gets noisy).
- Max ~1 SFX per 2–3s unless the edit is intentionally punchy; style.md may
  say "sfx on every card" — follow it.

## Music bed

```html
<audio src="assets/music.mp3" data-start="0" data-duration="42"
  data-media-start="12.0" data-volume="0.22"
  data-audio-fade-in="0.5" data-audio-fade-out="1.2"></audio>
```

- `data-volume` 0.15–0.3 under speech — audible but never competing.
- `data-media-start` trims into the track like video (`data-audio-start` is
  the legacy alias).
- Fade in ~0.3–0.8s, fade out ~1–2s at the tail. If the user gives an outro
  card, let the bed end just after it.

## Voice/audio hygiene

- `probe` the footage first: if it has no audio stream, say so instead of
  silently losing sound.
- Source audio stays on the `<video>` elements (default in scaffold); keep
  `data-has-audio` on. Music/SFX are separate `<audio>` elements mixed by the
  FFmpeg graph at render time — nothing to duck manually at these levels.
